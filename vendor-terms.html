Vendor terms

DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW. These terms are a plain-language placeholder written by the build team. They are not legal advice and have not been reviewed by a lawyer. They will change before real launch. Version: draft-1.

1. Who can sell

You can apply to sell if you run a real business or make or grow real products in Carriacou, Petite Martinique or Grenada. You must give true information. Each business needs one account owner who is responsible for it.

2. Approval

Every application is reviewed by Carriacou Connect before your shop is shown to shoppers. We may approve, ask for changes, or reject an application, and we give a reason. We may suspend a business that breaks these terms; a suspended business is hidden and cannot receive new orders, but can still see and finish its existing orders.

3. Your products and prices

You are responsible for your products being as described, legal to sell, safe, and correctly priced in Eastern Caribbean dollars (EC$). Food, beauty and similar products must meet any local health and labelling rules that apply to you.

3a. Photo labels: Real photo or Stock photo

Every product photo carries a label that shoppers can see: "Real photo" or "Stock photo – actual item may differ". You choose the label for each photo when you add it; there is no default, so you must pick one.

  • Real photo means a photo your shop took of the actual item you sell. Restaurants and food sellers: a Real photo must show the actual dish as served.
  • Stock photos are allowed if they are labelled "Stock photo". This includes supplier or catalogue pictures and images from the internet that you have the right to use.
  • Labels must be truthful. Labelling a stock, AI-generated or borrowed image as "Real photo" is a breach of these Terms.
  • Photos added by CSV import, and older photos that were never labelled, are shown as "Stock photo" until you relabel them.
  • Customers can report a photo labelled real that isn't, or that doesn't match the item. Carriacou Connect may relabel the photo, remove it, hide the listing, or for repeated or serious breaches suspend your shop. You will see a note in your dashboard explaining why.
  • A hint about missing camera information may appear for photos you mark Real. It is advice only and never blocks saving.

4. Orders, pickup and delivery

You agree to fulfil the orders you accept, keep customers informed using the order notes, and honour the pickup or delivery options you offer. Refund and cancellation handling is described in the order screens.

5. Tax

You are responsible for your own taxes, including VAT if you are required to be VAT registered. The tax settings in the dashboard only calculate what you ask them to; they are not tax advice.

6. Plans and fees

Plans (Lite, Standard, Plus+, Pro, Enterprise) and any platform fee are in test mode: nothing is billed yet. The prices and fees shown may change before launch and you will be told first.

7. Payments

Customers pay you directly (cash, card or bank transfer on pickup or delivery, or later through your own card merchant account). Carriacou Connect does not hold or move customers' money. The old play-money wallet is switched off.

7a. Platform Funds and fees

Platform Funds are prepaid credit you buy from Carriacou Connect (by card, or at the office in cash or by card). They can only be spent on Carriacou Connect's own services: your plan subscription, per-order platform fees, featured listings and ads, and setup or photo services. They cannot be sent to other users or cashed out. Unused Platform Funds are refunded only when you close your shop: the platform manager confirms the request and the refund is paid in the next pay cycle.

Per-order platform fee (proposed, not final): 1% of the order total when the customer pays online by card through a payment processor, and 2% when you are paid directly (pay on pickup or delivery). The fee is charged when the order is placed and billed at the end of the pay cycle from your Platform Funds; anything not covered is invoiced. If an order is cancelled or refunded, its fee is refunded (currently in full). In this preview all Platform Funds are test play money.

7b. CC Points and Carriacou Connect gift cards

Customers earn CC Points (1 point per EC$1 of completed orders). CC Points are rewards, not money. Carriacou Connect alone creates Carriacou Connect gift cards, sets their values and points prices, and chooses which shops each card applies to. Shops cannot create, price or sell these cards. Customers obtain them only by redeeming CC Points, never for money.

Opting in. Shops on qualifying plans (currently Plus+, Pro and Enterprise; to be confirmed) may opt in or out at any time from Platform Funds. Only shops that are opted in and on a qualifying plan appear in the Points Store. A card can only be used at the shop it was issued for, on Carriacou Connect.

Subscription credit, not cash. When a customer pays you with a Carriacou Connect gift card, Carriacou Connect credits the amount used against your plan subscription. You do not receive cash for redeemed cards. Credit above your subscription rolls over to later billing cycles and is refunded only as part of an exit refund if you close your shop. Each shop has a monthly gift card limit.

Opting out. Opting out stops new cards being issued for your shop. Cards that customers already hold for your shop must still be accepted until they are used or expire, and you will still receive subscription credit for them. You may also sell and honour your own gift cards; if you sell them through the platform, the normal transaction fees apply.

7c. Your own shop gift cards (gift card tracker)

On the Plus+, Pro and Enterprise plans you can use the gift card tracker to record gift cards that your shop sells or gives away, whether you sell them in person or through a normal order on Carriacou Connect.

Your shop alone is responsible for these gift cards and for the money paid for them. Carriacou Connect only keeps the record (code, value, balance, dates, buyer details you enter and the history of redemptions and changes). We do not sell, hold, pay out, refund or guarantee these cards, and they are separate from Carriacou Connect gift cards and CC Points (7b). You set and follow your own rules for expiry, refunds and lost cards, and you must follow consumer protection law that applies to you. Keep the buyer details you enter accurate and only as much as you need.

If your plan drops below Plus+, you can still look up, redeem and void your existing cards so you can honour them, but you cannot issue new ones. If you turn on the balance check on your shop page, customers who enter a code see only its balance, status and expiry.

8. Your information

The phone numbers, WhatsApp number, VAT number and business registration number you give in your application are seen only by Carriacou Connect staff to check your application. They are not shown on your public shop page. Your business name, description, area and products are public once you are approved.

9. Changes and ending

You may stop selling at any time. We may update these terms; we will ask you to accept new versions.

10. Ratings, reviews and community guidelines

Who can review. Only verified buyers: a customer whose order containing the product is completed or delivered. One review per item bought. The customer can edit it for 30 days.

What a review contains. A 1 to 5 star product rating, an optional 1 to 5 star shop service rating (delivery and communication), and an optional comment of up to 1000 characters. Text and stars only: no photos, images or image links in reviews or in shop replies. The system rejects image markup and links to images.

Guidelines for everyone. Be honest and about your own experience with the product or shop. No abuse, threats, hate, personal information (phone numbers, addresses), spam, advertising, or links to other shops. Do not offer rewards for good reviews or pressure customers to change a review. Do not review your own shop or a competitor.

Shops. You can post one public reply to each review and edit it. You cannot delete or hide reviews. If a review breaks these guidelines, report it.

Reports and moderation. Anyone signed in can report a review or a listing. Carriacou Connect checks reports and may hide or restore a review. A hidden review does not count in ratings. Limits apply to how often reviews and reports can be posted.

Rankings. Featured products are ordered by a weighted rating that gives little weight to products with few reviews, plus how recent the product or its reviews are. Products without reviews can still be featured.
